Sustainable Carbon Use

Sustainable solutions for current global challenges, such as the carbon budget, circular economy, human health, and clean energy, through synthetic biology. My research program will focus on developing microbes as green cell factories with tailor-made catalysts that can convert biomass-derived feedstocks into fuels, valuable commodities, or pharmaceutical products. This research is centered around two short- term goals: 1) Developing designer catalysts via enzyme evolution to enhance the metabolic process and create new carbon sinks, and 2) Engineering green factories with designer catalysts that can simultaneously degrade lignocellulosic waste and produce valuable commodities. Over the next five years, my research program will prioritize the development of energy-efficient enzymes and improvements in metabolic engineering efforts to convert available biomass into valuable commodities, paving the way for sustainable chemistry.
