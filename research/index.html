Research

Synthetic biology is one of the most exciting areas of science, allowing us to combine science and engineering to tackle complex, global challenges. The Joshi Lab at UBC is an interdisciplinary team of scientists focusing designing and improving microbes as green cell factories with tailor-made catalysts. We develop metabolic pathways to turn waste into valuable molecules, find and improve enzymes to catalyze desired reactions, and explore the questions needed for a sustainable future.

Explore our research projects below:

Metabolic engineering allows us to turn waste into treasure.

By pairing microbial cell factories to industrial waste sources we can divert carbon flow and generate valuable products while also contributing to a green economy.

Machine-learning aided design lets us tackle large problem with ease. Here we generate thousands of enzyme in silico before validating the most promising ones.

Continuous directed evolution allows us to explore large numbers of potential improvements.
