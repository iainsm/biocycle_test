BIOCYCLE

Paving the way for sustainable chemistry using metabolic engineering and synthetic biology.

At the BioCycle Lab, we harness synthetic biology, enzyme evolution, and metabolic engineering to create sustainable biotechnological solutions for global challenges in food, feed, and the circular bioeconomy. Our research focuses on designing long-life, high-performance enzymes — designer catalysts that can convert agricultural and forestry waste into biochemicals, biomaterials, and green fuels. By integrating continuous directed evolution, machine learning, and metabolic pathway design, we aim to build efficient microbial cell factories that transform waste into valuable resources — turning trash into treasure.
