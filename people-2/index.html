Jaya Joshi, Principal Investigator
Jaya Joshi is a synthetic biologist interested in the directed evolution of enzymes to transform inefficient designer biocatalysts into highly active enzymes. Her research walks through enzyme design space: exploring fitness landscapes for radical metabolic engineering design ideas, guided by the aim of successful implementation of carbon farming by 2050.
